## Full description

**Settle any argument with an impartial AI judge.**

DebateAI turns any disagreement into a real debate. Pick a topic — anything you
want — and two players make their case out loud (or by typing). After each turn,
an AI judge scores the argument on accuracy, strength, and clarity, fact-checks
it on the spot, and reads the verdict aloud.

**Three ways to play**
• **Pass & Play** — two players, one phone, quick rounds
• **Solo vs AI** — debate against an AI opponent (easy, medium, or hard)
• **Formal Debate** — opening statements, a real cross-examination, and closings, with a distinct voice for each speaker

**Why you'll like it**
• Debate literally any topic — no preset list
• Instant, impartial scoring and fact-checking
• Spoken verdicts that make every result feel like a real match
• Hands-free flow: a countdown, then it records, scores, and moves on
• Type instead of speaking anytime

Whether you're settling "pineapple on pizza" with a friend or sharpening your
arguments solo, DebateAI is the fastest way to find out who's actually right.
