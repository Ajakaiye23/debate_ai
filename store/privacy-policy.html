DebateAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

DebateAI ("the app", "we") is a debate game in which players speak or type arguments, an AI judges them, and a spoken verdict is read back. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • Your arguments — the audio you record and/or the text you type during a debate.
  • Debate content — the topic you enter, player names you choose, scores, and verdicts.
  • App settings — your preferences (e.g. chosen voice, volume, default rounds).

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or device identifiers, and the app has no user accounts.

How it's used and shared

To make the game work, your argument content is sent to third-party AI services:

  • OpenAI (Whisper) — to transcribe your spoken audio into text. (Only if you use voice input.)
  • Anthropic (Claude) — to score and fact-check your arguments and write the verdict.
  • ElevenLabs — to convert the verdict text into spoken audio. (Only if AI voices are enabled.)

These providers process the content to return a result and are governed by their own privacy policies (OpenAI, Anthropic, and ElevenLabs). We do not sell your data, show you ads, or use your debates for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your debate history and settings are stored only on your device. We do not keep a copy on our servers. Audio recordings are temporary — they are sent for transcription and not retained by the app. You can delete your debate history at any time from the app's Settings screen.

Children

DebateAI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Type instead of speaking to avoid sending audio entirely.
  • Disable AI voices to avoid using the text-to-speech service.
  • Clear all saved debates from Settings → Clear history.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the date above.
